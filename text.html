3. Text Formatting Tags

Bold text: Yeh text bold hai (b tag).

Strong text: Yeh important bold text hai (strong tag).

Italic text: Yeh text italic hai (i tag).

Emphasized text: Yeh emphasized text hai (em tag).

Underlined text: Yeh underlined text hai (u tag).

Highlighted text: Yeh highlighted text hai (mark tag).

Small text: Yeh chhota text hai (small tag).

Deleted text: Yeh kata hua text hai (del tag).

Inserted text: Yeh naya joda gaya text hai (ins tag).
